## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-14

### Added

- Email, IPv4, Luhn payment-card, credential assignment, and dictionary
  detectors with deterministic overlap resolution and exact allowlists.
- Keep, mask, remove, and deterministic token actions with profile parsing.
- Sanitized output, privacy-preserving scan/audit reports, named batches, and
  residual verification.
- One-based source locations, labeled-corpus precision/recall evaluation,
  policy comparison, and static policy linting.
- Portable CLI, runnable examples, multi-target tests, documentation, and CI.
